What we do

Most of what people come in for

If it hurts, chips, or has been sitting on your list for a year, it starts here.

01

Check-ups & cleaning

A look at gums, bite and decay, plus a scale and polish. The appointment most people should have twice a year.

02

Toothache & infection

Same-day slots for acute pain. We settle the infection first, then talk about the longer-term repair.

03

Fillings & root canal

Composite rather than metal, and treatment carried out with a rubber dam so nothing reaches the rest of your mouth.

04

Crowns & bridges

Ceramic onlays and crowns when a tooth is too broken to fill. We explain what is being replaced and why before we start.

05

Gum disease care

Deep cleaning with a clear plan afterwards. Gum problems are usually quiet for years, then they are not.

06

Whitening & veneers

Whitening trays to take home, and veneers only when a change in shape is the actual problem.

How we work

Three things we will not skip

You find out before we start

We show you the finding on the screen, name the options, and give you the cost. Nothing gets drilled until you have said yes.

It is okay to stop

A raised hand pauses everything, immediately and without a lecture. Many of our patients needed a few appointments before they managed the whole thing.

Prices before appointments

The estimate is the estimate. If what we find changes it, we stop and tell you, and you decide whether to continue.
