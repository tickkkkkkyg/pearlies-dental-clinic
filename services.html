Practice

What we treat, and what it involves

Every appointment starts with an examination and a written estimate. Nothing below happens without you agreeing to it first.

01

Check-ups & cleaning

A look at gums, bite and decay, plus a scale and polish. The appointment most people should have twice a year.

02

Toothache & infection

Same-day slots for acute pain. We settle the infection first, then talk about the longer-term repair.

03

Fillings & root canal

Composite rather than metal, and treatment carried out with a rubber dam so nothing reaches the rest of your mouth.

04

Crowns & bridges

Ceramic onlays and crowns when a tooth is too broken to fill. We explain what is being replaced and why before we start.

05

Gum disease care

Deep cleaning with a clear plan afterwards. Gum problems are usually quiet for years, then they are not.

06

Whitening & veneers

Whitening trays to take home, and veneers only when a change in shape is the actual problem.

07

Children's dentistry

Gentle first visits, fissure sealants and early orthodontic checks. We make the chair feel safe.

08

Emergency care

Same-day slots for broken, knocked-out or acutely painful teeth. Call the number on the contact page.
