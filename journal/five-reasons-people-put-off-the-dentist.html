Five reasons people put off the dentist

Cost, fear, bad memories, no symptoms, and not knowing where to start. Each one has a smaller answer than you would think.

Cost. The worry is usually bigger than the bill. A check-up is the cheapest appointment we offer, and it is the one that prevents the expensive ones. We also give written estimates before anything begins.

Fear. Raised hand, everything stops. That is the whole deal. We go at your pace, explain as we go, and never rush.

Bad memories. Dentistry has changed. Numbing is better, drills are quieter, and we use rubber dam and magnification as standard. If your last visit was ten years ago, it is a different experience now.

No symptoms. Decay and gum disease do not hurt until they are advanced. Feeling fine is not the same as being fine.

Not knowing where to start. Send a short note. We will reply with a slot and a rough idea of cost. That is all it takes.
