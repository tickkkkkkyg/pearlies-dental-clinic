What your toothpaste is actually doing

Fluoride is the whole argument. Everything else on the shelf is marketing with a small amount of benefit mixed in.

Toothpaste does two things that matter: it delivers fluoride to the enamel, and it removes plaque through mild abrasion. Everything else — whitening, charcoal, herbal extracts, "natural" formulas — is decoration around those two jobs.

Fluoride remineralises early decay and makes enamel harder to dissolve. It is the single most effective thing you can do for your teeth besides brushing. If your toothpaste has fluoride and you use it twice a day, you are doing the important part.

Whitening toothpastes work by abrasion, which can wear enamel over time and rarely delivers the dramatic result on the box. Charcoal is worse — it is abrasive and has no fluoride benefit at all.

The boring advice is the right advice: a fluoride toothpaste, a soft brush, two minutes, twice a day. Spend the money you saved on the fancy tube on a good electric brush instead.
