About

A small practice that takes its time

We opened in 2014 with one chair and the belief that dentistry should feel less like a transaction and more like a conversation. We have grown to three chairs and a team of six, but the way we work has not changed.

Our values

What we will not compromise on

Honesty first

We tell you what we see, what it means, and what it costs. If a tooth does not need treatment, we say so.

Your pace

You are in control of the speed. We can stop, split treatment across visits, or take a break whenever you need.

Modern, careful work

Digital imaging, rubber dam, magnification and materials chosen for longevity — not for the cheapest invoice.
